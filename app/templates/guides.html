Planning guidance across the UK

Planning law is devolved, so Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland each have their own system. These official sites cover the detail for each nation. PlanFind searches councils in all four.

England

  • Planning Portal
  • National Planning Policy Framework
  • Planning Inspectorate (appeals)
  • Historic England

Wales

  • Planning Portal (England and Wales)
  • Welsh Government: planning appeals
  • Cadw (historic environment)

Scotland

  • mygov.scot: how to apply
  • Scottish Government: development management
  • Planning and Environmental Appeals (DPEA)
  • Historic Environment Scotland

Northern Ireland

  • NI Planning Portal (nidirect)
  • Department for Infrastructure: planning portal
  • Planning Appeals Commission
