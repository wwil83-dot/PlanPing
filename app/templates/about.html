What is PlanFind?

PlanFind collects planning application data directly from council planning registers across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, so you can search by postcode and set up email alerts for your local area.

It's free, independent, and needs no account to search.

Why we built it

Finding out about planning applications near your home is surprisingly difficult. Councils publish the information, but it's buried in hard-to-navigate portals — and by the time a neighbour's letter arrives, the consultation window may already be closing.

PlanFind fixes that: search your postcode, see what's been submitted nearby, and get notified the moment something new appears.

Where does the data come from?

We connect directly to each council's own online planning register — the same public system your council already publishes through — and check it on a regular schedule, so you don't need to visit dozens of council websites yourself.

Most areas are checked nightly. A small number of sources are checked slightly less often, based on how each council's system responds to automated requests — we'd rather check reliably every few days than risk a source becoming unavailable by checking too aggressively.

Where a council doesn't yet publish in a format we can connect to directly, we also draw on planning.data.gov.uk, the government's national planning data platform, and, for biodiversity gain sites, the Defra / Natural England Biodiversity Gain Sites Register. All data is used under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Is it really free?

Yes — searching and basic weekly email alerts are completely free, with no account needed. Just enter your postcode and go.

We plan to introduce optional paid tiers for instant alerts and wider-radius searches, but the core service will always be free.

How do I object to an application?

Every application links directly to the council's planning portal, where you can read the documents and submit a formal comment or objection.

Consultation windows typically run around 21 days from validation, but vary by council, application type and nation — always check the dates on the council's own page.
