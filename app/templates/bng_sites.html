What this data shows Each pin is a real site registered on the government's official Biodiversity Gain Sites Register — its size, habitat type, and which developments (if any) have drawn biodiversity units from it. This is genuine, official data, sourced directly from the register under the Open Government Licence.
What this data does not show The register does not publish prices, whether a site's units are currently for sale, or landowner contact details. The "registering body" shown for each site is the legal party holding the site's Section 106 agreement or conservation covenant — not necessarily who to contact about buying units. This page is a starting point for research, not a marketplace — every site links through to the official government register for further detail.
