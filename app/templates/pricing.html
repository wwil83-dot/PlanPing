Frequently asked questions

Short answers about what stays free, what is coming and what we do with your email.

Ask us a question
Will search stay free?

Yes. Searching by postcode and getting email alerts near your home are free, and we have no plan to change that.

Will anything I use now become paid?

Search and email alerts near your home stay free. Bulk search and CSV export are free during launch, and some limits will apply once the paid plans open. We will announce it on this page and by email first.

When will the paid plans open?

We have not set a date. We will build them once enough people on the waitlist ask for the same things.

Will I be charged for joining?

No. The waitlist is only an email address. You will not be asked for card details until a plan opens, and you can say no then.

Where does the data come from?

Directly from council planning registers across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, refreshed every night. See which councils we cover.
